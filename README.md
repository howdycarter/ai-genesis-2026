# AI GENESIS 2026 — Entry

## Objective
Win a prize at AI GENESIS 2026 — "The Largest AI Hackathon In The Middle East"
(lablab.ai × /function1, hybrid: online + Dubai finale) — with an AI app that
scores on all four judging criteria: Application of Technology, Presentation,
Business Value, Originality.

## Source of Truth
- Competition rules: `docs/competition.md` (deep-researched from the official
  lablab.ai event page, Oct 12 2026)
- Win plan: `docs/win-plan.md`
- Product concepts: `docs/prds/` (one PRD per likely partner track)
- Official site: https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/ai-genesis-2026

## Done When
- [ ] Registered on lablab.ai + joined the lablab.ai Discord (before kickoff stream)
- [ ] Concept + partner track locked after the 2026 partner reveal
- [ ] Working app deployed to a public URL (Vercel)
- [ ] Public GitHub repo with all source, run instructions, MIT license (this repo)
- [ ] Demo video (MP4, under 5 min) — problem → solution → tech → business → roadmap
- [ ] Slide deck (PDF)
- [ ] lablab.ai submission complete with correct partner tag before the deadline
- [ ] Chris explicitly approves the submission before anything is posted

## Acceptance Criteria
- Partner technology is the engine of the build, not a logo on the landing page
- Judges can use a working build at a public URL — a mockup does not qualify
- Video shows the product working end to end on real inputs
- Business case is concrete: who pays, how much, market size, competitors

## Constraints
- Code must be original work, open source, MIT-compliant (this repo is MIT)
- Public repo is mandatory — everything here is public by design
- Nothing is submitted, published, or posted without Chris's explicit approval
- Plagiarism or gaming → immediate disqualification

## Non-Goals
- Production-grade infra (working prototype / proof of concept)
- Winning at the expense of the core concept (no prize-chasing pivots mid-build)

## Verifier
Chris reviews the live demo and the video, then approves the submission.
