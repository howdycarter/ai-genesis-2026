## Rules

**Submission must include** (per the lablab.ai Hackathon Rule Book):
- Title, short description, long description, technology + **partner category tags**
- Cover image (PNG/JPG, 16:9)
- **Video (MP4)** — under 5 minutes, covering: problem, solution, value proposition,
  market analysis, revenue model, competitors, future plans
- **Slide deck (PDF)**
- **Public GitHub repository** (mandatory)
- **Working application URL** — deploy on Streamlit, Replit, or Vercel

**Judging criteria** (each scored 1–5, no published weights):
1. **Application of Technology** — how effectively the chosen model(s) are integrated
   (demo video + working link + GitHub quality)
2. **Presentation** — clarity and effectiveness of the project presentation
3. **Business Value** — impact, practical/commercial value, market fit
4. **Originality** — uniqueness and creativity of the solution

**Integrity:**
- All submissions must be **original work, open source, MIT License-compliant**.
  Public repos are mandatory — submissions are inherently public.
- Plagiarism, cheating, system tampering, unauthorized automation, or gaming
  voting → **immediate disqualification**.
- Manual submission allowed up to 6 hours after deadline with prior organizer approval.
- No explicit banned-tech list. Any models/libraries allowed — but the **chosen
  partner's technology must be central, not decorative**.
- No dedicated IP-assignment clause found — but assume everything submitted is public.

## What wins (from 2023–2026 lablab.ai winners)

- **Real-world, high-impact problems** — healthcare, accessibility, finance, commerce.
  Toy demos don't win.
- **Deep partner-tech integration** — judged *within the partner category*; the
  partner's API/model/platform must be the core of the build.
- **Working, deployed demo** — live URL + video + public repo are mandatory and scored.
- **Business case** — rubric rewards market analysis, revenue model, future plans.
  Judges: top builds were "viable businesses, not just prototypes."
- **Strong sub-5-minute pitch video** — problem → solution → value prop → market →
  competitors → next steps.
- **Solo builders win** — explicitly allowed; multiple solo 1st places on record.
- **Agentic/workflow systems** with realistic data, auditability, and
  human-in-the-loop checkpoints are favored in recent editions.
